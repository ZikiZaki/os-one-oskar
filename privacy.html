Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

OS-ONE Oskar is a private, single-user assistant. It accesses only the Google account of its owner, with the owner's consent.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail: reading messages and creating drafts. It never sends email.
  • Google Calendar: reading and managing events.
  • Google Drive: reading files.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to help the owner with their own tasks. It is processed on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models. Access credentials are kept in an encrypted password vault.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
